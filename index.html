Our Legacy

Over 30 Years of Optical Excellence

Founded in 1992, Aloka Opticians has transformed into Sri Lanka’s premier destination for vision and hearing care.

Our mission is to bring world-class clinical care and stylish, comfortable eyewear to Sri Lanka through innovation and personal service.

High-quality care, backed by real expertise
